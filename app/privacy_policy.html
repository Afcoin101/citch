Effective Date: July 28, 2026

Welcome to Citch ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Citch (Package Name: com.aistudio.homechef.vygqpk / com.citch).

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways when you use the app:

  • Personal Account Data: When you register, sign in, or update your profile, we may collect your name, email address, profile picture, and account credentials.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we collect precise or approximate location information from your mobile device to help you discover nearby home chefs, kitchens, and local food services.
  • Usage & Device Information: We automatically collect information about your mobile device, including hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and app usage logs to improve performance.
  • Notification Tokens: We collect Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to send you real-time notifications regarding order updates, kitchen status, and messaging alerts.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various business and operational purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the features and functionality of the Citch application.
  • To match you with nearby home chefs, local kitchens, and delivery services based on location.
  • To send order notifications, account alerts, and administrative updates.
  • To verify user identity and prevent fraudulent activities or misuse.
  • To process support requests and respond to user inquiries.

3. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party service providers that process data on our behalf to operate the application:

  • Google Firebase: Used for authentication, cloud database storage (Firestore), real-time database, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push notifications.
  • Google Maps / Location Services: Used to display maps, nearby kitchen distances, and location-based discovery.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only under the following circumstances:

  • With Kitchens & Chefs: Relevant order details (such as items requested, delivery address, or contact number) are shared with the chef or kitchen fulfilling your order.
  • Service Providers: Secure infrastructure providers like Google Firebase for app functionality.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, subpoena, or legal process to protect user safety or enforce our terms of service.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. Users have the right to request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

6. Security of Your Information

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures (including SSL encryption and secure Firebase authentication rules) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

7. Children's Privacy
