# Privacy Policy - Citch App

**Last Updated:** July 20, 2026

Welcome to **Citch** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about our policy or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us.

This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our mobile application, **Citch**, and any related services, sales, marketing, or events.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering on the App, expressing an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and services, or when participating in activities on the App.

### Personal Information Provided by You
* **Profile Credentials:** Name, email address, physical shipping address for food deliveries, and phone number.
* **Payment Information:** We utilize third-party billing providers (such as **Stripe**) to handle credit card transactions securely. We do not store or process raw credit card numbers or banking passwords on our local servers.

### Information Automatically Collected
* **Location Data:** We request access to fine and coarse geographic coordinates from your device to identify nearby Home Chefs, calculate accurate delivery paths, and simulate real-time tracking of courier deliveries.
* **Local Room Database State:** To allow high-performance, responsive offline capabilities, food cart items, historical order receipts, and custom culinary preferences are securely stored locally on your Android device using SQLite (Android Room Database).
* **Device Information:** Standard diagnostics such as operating system version, unique device identifiers, and basic network connection metrics.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information for purposes based on legitimate business interests, the fulfillment of our contract with you, compliance with our legal obligations, and/or your consent:
1. **To Facilitate Order Checkout & Processing:** Using Stripe SDK and secure backend integrations to complete payment processing for custom food orders.
2. **To Provide Real-time Delivery Tracking:** Leveraging device GPS sensors and Google Maps API coordinates to track active courier locations in real-time.
3. **AI Culinary Assistant Features:** Processing menu preferences using server-side Gemini API sandboxes to recommend specialized cooking tutorials, ingredient modifications, and nutritional plans.
4. **To manage User Accounts:** Allowing customizable user experiences, profile management, and contact logs.

---

## 3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We only share information with your consent, to comply with laws, to provide you with services, to protect your rights, or to fulfill business obligations:
* **Third-Party Service Providers:** We share billing tokens with Stripe to securely execute payments. We pass geographic routing queries to Google Maps APIs for spatial indexing and map renders.
* **Professional Home Chefs:** Necessary delivery addresses and contact names are shared with registered home chefs to complete culinary preparations and shipments.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Security

We keep your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures (including secure SSL network communication and local sandbox isolation) designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, please also remember that we cannot guarantee that the internet itself is 100% secure.

---

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have specific rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, rectification, or deletion of the data stored on our systems. You can manage your profile records or delete your local database storage history directly inside the application's Go-Live configuration settings screen.
